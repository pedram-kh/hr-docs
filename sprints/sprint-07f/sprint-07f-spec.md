> **The last sprint of 7.** Two jobs that only make sense together: **(0)** run the reference-fact flow on staging for real, so facts exist to scope; **(1–3)** make group scope *structured* — a real group/sub-area on the fact and on the employee, compared exactly — with the structure **proposed by AI and approved by a human**, and **no AI at answer time** (ADR-0015/0016: the model never makes the scope decision). Granularity follows the convenio: a group is split into sub-areas **only where the convenio assigns them different values.**

## Goal
Replace prose-in-`group_label` digit matching with an exact comparison on **structured group scope** that exists on both the fact and the employee — populated by AI-propose-then-human-verify on top of the salary-minted category vocabulary — so a group-scoped verified fact answers the right employee and never the wrong one, and an unresolved group still escalates. Precondition: the reference-fact flow runs end-to-end on staging first (segment → verify → answer), because there is nothing to scope until it does.

## Phase 1 — Structured group scope (the model)
- **Extend, don't replace, the category vocabulary.** `convenio_job_categories` (94 rows, salary-minted, with `group_code`) is the base. Add a structured **group** notion on top: a `convenio_groups` table (or an additive shape on categories — the plan decides against the real schema) with `convenio_id`, `group_code` (normalized: `1`, `2`, `3`, `I`…), `label` (as printed), and an optional **`sub_area`** discriminator (e.g. `área 5`, `resto áreas`) — present **only** where the convenio assigns different values to sub-areas. A category belongs to a group; a group may have sub-areas; the **scope key** a fact/employee carries is `(convenio_id, group_id[, sub_area_id])`.
- **Facts bind to structured scope.** `reference_facts` gains nullable `group_id` + `sub_area_id` (additive); `group_label` stays as the printed provenance string. The 7b-2 segmentation agent's output is **mapped** to structured scope through the propose flow below (the agent still emits `group_label` verbatim; the *binding* is a proposal a human approves).
- **Employees get a structured group.** `employees` gains nullable `group_id` + `sub_area_id` (additive), exposed in the directory create/edit + CSV bootstrap as FK pickers scoped to the employee's convenio (ADR-0011: existing vocabulary only). A blank means *unresolved* → the matcher escalates, exactly as today. **No auto-assignment of employees** — HR sets it, or it stays blank.
- **Job category → group is deterministic where the salary table already says so** (`group_code` on the category); the sub-area is never inferred from a category.

## Phase 2 — AI proposes the group structure; a human approves (ADR-0020 spine)
- A propose job (`ProposeConvenioGroups`, mirroring `ProposeDocumentTags`/`SegmentReferenceSource`): hr-ai `POST /propose-groups` reads the convenio's text (`document_pages`) **and** its existing categories + any `reference_facts.group_label` values, and returns a **proposed group structure**: groups, their printed labels, the categories that belong to each, and **sub-areas only where the text assigns different values** (with the source excerpt that justifies the split — e.g. the periodo de prueba lines that give área 5 vs resto áreas different numbers). Closed-set-validated against the convenio's categories; never mints categories; writes nothing (ADR-0007).
- Proposals land inert (`needs_review`, `ai_agent`, fuchsia) on a **Groups** review surface: per convenio, the proposed tree, the justifying excerpts, the categories mapped, and the facts whose `group_label` would bind to each node. Human actions: **approve / edit / reject** per group and per sub-area (append-only provenance). Approval creates the structured rows and **binds the matching verified facts** (`group_label` → `group_id`/`sub_area_id`) — shown as a diff the human confirms, never silent.
- **Eval (the 7b-2 discipline):** a gold group structure for the convenios in the fixtures (Navarra Hostelería with its área-5 split; COEAS Álava/Andalucía/Estatal G1–G6 unsplit; Navarra Intervención Social G1/G2) scored for *proposed the split exactly where values differ / over-split / under-split*. Over-splitting is safe-ish (escalates more); **under-splitting is the failure** (merges two values into one group → wrong answer). Report it.

## Phase 3 — The exact matcher (the answer-side change, narrow)
- `ReferenceFactAnswerService` Tier 2 compares **structured scope**, not digits: employee `(group_id, sub_area_id)` vs fact `(group_id, sub_area_id)`. Rules: exact group + exact sub-area → match; fact has a sub-area and the employee's is blank → **escalate** (can't tell which slice); fact has no sub-area (undivided group) and employee group matches → match (the simple one-level case, preserved); no group on employee → Tier 3 convenio-wide else escalate (unchanged). **`factMatchesGroup` digit-regex is deleted.** Facts still carrying only a `group_label` (unbound) are **not matchable by group** — they fall to convenio-wide-or-escalate until bound.
- Everything else in 7c is untouched: the pre-check, only-verified, validity, `structured_reference` authority, skip-ground on P1, composition on P2. The golden-trace regression stays green; the 7c invariant suite gains the new cases.
- **Tests (the ones that matter):** (a) the exact bug — employee Grupo 2 *resto áreas* + facts 202/203 → gets **60/45/30**, never 90/75/60; (b) employee Grupo 2 with blank sub-area → **escalates**; (c) employee Grupo 1 → 90/75/60; (d) undivided group (Álava G1) matches on group alone; (e) an unbound `group_label`-only fact never matches by group; (f) the digit regex is gone (a fact labelled "Grupo 12" can't match group 1 or 2).

## Risks / notes
- **Under-splitting is the dangerous error** (merges different values); over-splitting only escalates more. Bias the proposal prompt toward splitting on any value difference, and measure it.
- **The employee side is a data-entry burden for HR** — a group picker on 1,500 profiles. Keep it optional (blank = escalate), derive the group from `job_category.group_code` where the salary table already says it, and note the CSV column for bulk load.
- **Don't let Phase 0 balloon.** It is a rerun of a proven flow; if the eval drifts on staging, that's a finding to record and fix narrowly, not a re-do of 7b-2.
