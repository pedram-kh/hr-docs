# ADR-0038 — The general lane may state that a legal figure exists; it may never state that it exists or applies *for the reader*. A synthesis abstention is a structured flag, not a phrase

**Status:** accepted (Slice 13c, CP-1 read list passed 2026-10-01 — `sprints/sprint-13c/eval/results/cp1-read-list.md`; abstention flag added the same day after the S3c finding, `sprints/sprint-13c/eval/results/s3d-report.md`).

## Context

The 13c lane (`HR_GENERAL_LANE_ENABLED` + `HR_GENERAL_LANE_MODEL_KNOWLEDGE`) answers a concept question from model knowledge, with no corpus citation, a fixed caveat (`ChatService::GENERAL_LANE_MODEL_CAVEAT`, `app/Services/ChatService.php:122`) and the deterministic post-check (`GeneralLanePostCheck::PATTERNS`, `app/Services/Agent/Rules/GeneralLanePostCheck.php:66`). Two things came out of S2–S3c.

**A. What may a model-knowledge draft say about a legal figure?** The CP-1 read list printed ten drafts in full — six that talk *around* a figure without asserting it (NEG-13, NEG-17, NEG-12, NEG-04, ln-12, ln-06: "no es una figura general regulada de forma uniforme…", "la normativa laboral regula los supuestos…") and four that affirm a figure's *existence* (HO-02, HO-08, HO-10, HO-07: "Existe la figura del permiso retribuido por motivos familiares…", "La indemnización … es una compensación económica que se puede abonar…"). Pedram read all ten and passed them.

**B. How is a synthesis abstention recognised?** S3c: LP-14 and LP-45 were persisted as the *corpus* answer ("No dispongo de información suficiente…") and the lane never opened (`s3c-report.md`). Root cause, in `s3d-report.md`: `CorpusMiss::isSynthesisAbstention()` was **structural**, not a phrase match — it required `check_b_citations=false`. The model abstained in prose **while still citing a related source** (`… solo mencionan a la Inspección de Trabajo [Fuente 1]`), which passes Check B, so the turn went down the answer path. That violates rule 6 of the `/synthesise` citation contract (abstain ⇒ `cited_sources: []`, confidence ≤ 0.2) and is non-deterministic (the same question abstained cleanly in a diagnostic run).

## Decision

1. **The existence rule.** The lane may state that a legal figure **exists** (a *concept*: "existe el permiso retribuido por motivos familiares", "la indemnización es una compensación que se puede abonar"). It may **never** state that the figure exists or applies **for the reader**: no second-person entitlement or obligation, no "te corresponde", no "tienes derecho", no "la empresa debe", no amount, duration, threshold or age. Concept yes; entitlement, applicability and numbers no. The deterministic layers enforce it (E1/E2 entitlement shapes, F1–F3/D1 figures, `ModelKnowledgeShapePostCallRule`); the lane prompt only *defines the concept* (`sprints/sprint-13c/plan.md:121`) and the caveat sends the reader to the convenio or HR for the application. The ten CP-1 drafts are the worked examples of both sides of the line.
2. **Structured abstention.** `/synthesise` accepts an opt-in `report_abstention: true` (`hr-ai/app/main.py:228`) and returns `abstained` (bool) and `abstained_by` (`model_flag` | `phrase`). The prompt addendum that asks for the flag (`hr-ai/app/providers/claude.py:137`) is appended to the system prompt **only when requested**, so the base prompt hash, requests and responses are byte-identical with the sub-flag off. The model's boolean wins; a missing boolean falls back to the opening-sentence phrase (`claude.py:148`, anchored — an answer that merely *ends* with "no dispongo…" is not an abstention); a disagreement is recorded (`abstention_phrase_disagrees`).
3. **The backend opens the lane on the flag.** `ProsePath` requests the flag only when `GuardrailPolicy::generalLaneModelKnowledgeEnabled()`, treats a flagged abstention as a failed floor (`synthesis abstained (flag)`, `escalate/low_confidence`) and records `floor_decision.synthesis_abstained`; `CorpusMiss::isSynthesisAbstention()` recognises that key first (the structural form stays as the fallback). `CorpusMiss` stays a whitelist: the sub-flag gates it, `fallback` turns are excluded.
4. **The gate counts an abstention as `abstain`, never `answer`.** `answer:gate` rows carry `outcome_class` (`abstain` | the raw outcome) and `abstained_by` (`flag` | `phrase`); `answers`, `outcomes` and `by_class` use it (`app/Console/Commands/AnswerGate.php:515`, `:723`). A turn that asked for the flag and got `abstained=false` is not overridden by the phrase. The hard `false_answer` check is unchanged.
5. **Tickets (not in this slice).** HO-07's `una vez` ("…hasta la reincorporación **una vez** recibida el alta") is blocked by **F2** (`"una vez"` is a spelled number + quantity noun, `GeneralLanePostCheck.php:71`); it joins the E2 definitional false positives (`garantizar`, `corresponde`: LP-01, LP-65, LP-75) on the **F2 / fresh-bank ticket**. E2 is unchanged.

## Consequences

- With the sub-flag on, the classic engine also escalates a flagged abstention instead of answering "no dispongo…" — rule 6 as intended.
- A partial answer (content, then "no contienen una definición…" at the end) is *not* an abstention by the anchored phrase rule.
- Lane off / sub-flag off: no `report_abstention` in the request, no `abstained` in the response, no new trace keys (17 tests, `tests/Feature/Sprint13cSynthesisAbstentionTest.php`, plus `hr-ai/scripts/synthesis_abstention_test.py`; full suite 1389/1389, goldens unchanged).
