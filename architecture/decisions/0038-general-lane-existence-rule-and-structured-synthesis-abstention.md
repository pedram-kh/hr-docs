# ADR-0038 — The general lane may state that a legal figure exists; it may never state that it exists or applies *for the reader*. A synthesis abstention is a structured flag, not a phrase

**Status:** accepted (Slice 13c, CP-1 read list passed 2026-10-01 — `sprints/sprint-13c/eval/results/cp1-read-list.md`; abstention flag added the same day after the S3c finding, `sprints/sprint-13c/eval/results/s3d-report.md`).

## Context

The 13c lane (`HR_GENERAL_LANE_ENABLED` + `HR_GENERAL_LANE_MODEL_KNOWLEDGE`) answers a concept question from model knowledge, with no corpus citation, a fixed caveat (`ChatService::GENERAL_LANE_MODEL_CAVEAT`, `app/Services/ChatService.php:122`) and the deterministic post-check (`GeneralLanePostCheck::PATTERNS`, `app/Services/Agent/Rules/GeneralLanePostCheck.php:66`). Two things came out of S2–S3c.

**A. What may a model-knowledge draft say about a legal figure?** The CP-1 read list printed ten drafts in full — six that talk *around* a figure without asserting it (NEG-13, NEG-17, NEG-12, NEG-04, ln-12, ln-06: "no es una figura general regulada de forma uniforme…", "la normativa laboral regula los supuestos…") and four that affirm a figure's *existence* (HO-02, HO-08, HO-10, HO-07: "Existe la figura del permiso retribuido por motivos familiares…", "La indemnización … es una compensación económica que se puede abonar…"). Pedram read all ten and passed them.

**B. How is a synthesis abstention recognised?** S3c: LP-14 and LP-45 were persisted as the *corpus* answer ("No dispongo de información suficiente…") and the lane never opened (`s3c-report.md`). Root cause, in `s3d-report.md`: `CorpusMiss::isSynthesisAbstention()` was **structural**, not a phrase match — it required `check_b_citations=false`. The model abstained in prose **while still citing a related source** (`… solo mencionan a la Inspección de Trabajo [Fuente 1]`), which passes Check B, so the turn went down the answer path. That violates rule 6 of the `/synthesise` citation contract (abstain ⇒ `cited_sources: []`, confidence ≤ 0.2) and is non-deterministic (the same question abstained cleanly in a diagnostic run).

## Decision

1. **The existence rule.** The lane may state that a legal figure **exists** (a *concept*: "existe el permiso retribuido por motivos familiares", "la indemnización es una compensación que se puede abonar"). It may **never** state that the figure exists or applies **for the reader**: no second-person entitlement or obligation, no "te corresponde", no "tienes derecho", no "la empresa debe", no amount, duration, threshold or age. Concept yes; entitlement, applicability and numbers no. The deterministic layers enforce it (E1/E2 entitlement shapes, F1–F3/D1 figures, `ModelKnowledgeShapePostCallRule`); the lane prompt only *defines the concept* (`sprints/sprint-13c/plan.md:121`) and the caveat sends the reader to the convenio or HR for the application. The ten CP-1 drafts are the worked examples of both sides of the line.
2. **Structured abstention.** `/synthesise` accepts an opt-in `report_abstention: true` (`hr-ai/app/main.py:228`) and returns `abstained` (bool) and `abstained_by` (`model_flag` | `phrase`). The prompt addendum that asks for the flag (`hr-ai/app/providers/claude.py:137`) is appended to the system prompt **only when requested**, so the base prompt hash, requests and responses are byte-identical with the sub-flag off. The model's boolean wins; a missing boolean falls back to the opening-sentence phrase (`claude.py:148`, anchored — an answer that merely *ends* with "no dispongo…" is not an abstention); a disagreement is recorded (`abstention_phrase_disagrees`).
3. **The backend opens the lane on the flag.** `ProsePath` requests the flag only when `GuardrailPolicy::generalLaneModelKnowledgeEnabled()`, treats a flagged abstention as a failed floor (`synthesis abstained (flag)`, `escalate/low_confidence`) and records `floor_decision.synthesis_abstained`; `CorpusMiss::isSynthesisAbstention()` recognises that key first (the structural form stays as the fallback). `CorpusMiss` stays a whitelist: the sub-flag gates it, `fallback` turns are excluded.
4. **The gate counts an abstention as `abstain`, never `answer`.** `answer:gate` rows carry `outcome_class` (`abstain` | the raw outcome) and `abstained_by` (`flag` | `phrase`); `answers`, `outcomes` and `by_class` use it (`app/Console/Commands/AnswerGate.php:515`, `:723`). A turn that asked for the flag and got `abstained=false` is not overridden by the phrase. The hard `false_answer` check is unchanged.
5. **Tickets (not in this slice).** HO-07's `una vez` ("…hasta la reincorporación **una vez** recibida el alta") is blocked by **F2** (`"una vez"` is a spelled number + quantity noun, `GeneralLanePostCheck.php:71`); it joins the E2 definitional false positives (`garantizar`, `corresponde`: LP-01, LP-65, LP-75) on the **F2 / fresh-bank ticket**. E2 is unchanged.

## Consequences

- With the sub-flag on, the classic engine also escalates a flagged abstention instead of answering "no dispongo…" — rule 6 as intended.
- A partial answer (content, then "no contienen una definición…" at the end) is *not* an abstention by the anchored phrase rule.
- Lane off / sub-flag off: no `report_abstention` in the request, no `abstained` in the response, no new trace keys (17 tests, `tests/Feature/Sprint13cSynthesisAbstentionTest.php`, plus `hr-ai/scripts/synthesis_abstention_test.py`; full suite 1389/1389, goldens unchanged).

## Amendment 2026-10-05 (Correction-13c-01) — the lane is a property of the question, not of the account

**Finding.** "¿Qué es un permiso PIF?" got a lane answer for `test-navarra@` (convenio with active text) and a `low_confidence` card for `test-fullgap@` (convenio 7, never ingested). The question was the same; the hand-over differed because of the *account*: `ProsePath` stamps `floor_decision.fallback = estatuto_gap` on a never-ingested turn, and `CorpusMiss` treated that marker as a reason to keep the abstention out of the lane. Four more states had the same shape (below). None of them says anything about the *question*.

**Decision.** The general-lane hand-over depends on exactly two things: **(a)** the question passes the explanatory gate (the v1 pre-screen, or the fail-closed v2 allow-list with the sub-flag on) and **(b)** the corpus did not answer. No account state — never ingested, expired, scope under review, no chunks, no group — is consulted. A non-explanatory question in any of those states keeps its existing escalation reason, byte for byte; with the lane off nothing changes at all.

**Untouched.** The sensitive-topic and other pre-model guards (they run before the loop), the off-domain decline (ADR-0039), the "convenio answers first" precedence (`NationalLawPrecedenceRule`; and a corpus *answer* still wins — the lane is only ever reached after a miss), `GeneralLanePostCheck`, `ModelKnowledgeShapePostCallRule`, the caveat and the badge. Still escalating: the aggregation guard, a post-Check-A figure-guard / Check-B / provider-error / truncated-grounding verdict, a composition conflict and a `same_validity_conflict` between verified facts (a conflict is not an absence). `salary_coverage_gap` / `profile_incomplete` are not touched.

**Consequences.**

- **What an explanatory question now meets, per state:** never ingested → Estatuto retrieval, then lane on an abstention / entailment failure (a Check-A miss already opened it); expired / under review / no text → `convenio_search` hands over without retrieval; no group → `reference_fact` hands over, then `convenio_search` runs (below).
- **A real behaviour change, not only a lane change.** For a topic with a verified fact and an employee whose group is unknown, an explanatory question used to end at the fact gap. It now continues to `convenio_search`, so the *convenio prose* may answer it ("convenio answers first") before the lane is considered. Entitlement questions still end at the gap.
- When the lane does not answer (the planner finalizes, the post-check or shape check discards it, the web draft fails and the model draft fails), the finisher re-surfaces the stashed corpus escalation: the same card as today.
- The lane is agent-engine only (the classic engine has no lane), so classic behaviour is untouched.
- Tests: `tests/Feature/Correction13c01LaneIsAPropertyOfTheQuestionTest.php` (25) — one group per removed state (and the composition hand-over), each asserting explanatory → lane opens and entitlement → today's reason with the lane absent, plus lane off byte-identical and the end-to-end loop. Three assertions that encoded the old exclusion were rewritten (`Sprint13GeneralLanePreconditionTest`, `Sprint13cLaneOpeningTest`, `Sprint13cSynthesisAbstentionTest`). Full suite 1587/1587, golden traces unchanged.
- **Cost of row 9:** a first-turn explanatory question whose composition abstains now pays the composition's LLM calls twice (round 0 decides to defer; `reference_fact` re-runs it), then `convenio_search`, then the lane. Accepted: it is only the miss path.
- **Known limit of model-knowledge answers — shape-checked, not truth-checked.** The lane's checks (pre-screen, post-check for figures / durations / entitlement language, shape check, the caveat and the badge) decide what an answer may *look like*; nothing decides whether a definition is *true*. On the gate re-run, `test-navarra@` (message 8561) answered "¿Qué es un permiso PIF?" with "Protección Integral frente a la violencia, también vinculado a permisos por motivos de fuerza mayor familiar". PIF is *Permiso Individual de Formación*. `test-fullgap@` and the expired-convenio account expanded it correctly in the same run. All locks passed, the caveat was present, and the badge showed; the answer reached the employee as designed. This is the accepted cost of "the lane is a property of the question": more questions reach model knowledge, so more of its errors do too. It is bounded by (a) the caveat that says the text was written "sin consultar tu convenio ni la normativa cargada y sin una fuente verificable", (b) the badge and the escalate button, and (c) Calidad sampling (below).
- **Mitigation — ticketed, not built: Slice 13f, an HR-curated acronym / term glossary in Guardarraíles.** HR maintains a short list of terms and acronyms the company uses (PIF, IT, ERTE, …) with the correct expansion and a one-line gloss; the lane prompt receives the entries that match the question as *given facts* the model must use and not contradict. The shape of the answer is unchanged; the glossary only removes the guesswork about what the acronym stands for. Ticket in `roadmap.md` §7. It does not make the lane truth-checked in general.
- **Calidad samples lane answers.** A lane answer is an answered turn (`floor_decision.outcome = answer`, `path = general_knowledge`, `authority_used = [general_knowledge]`), so `QualitySamplingService::draw()` takes it from `DeflectionAnalytics::liveTurns()` like any other. It forms its own `(path, territory)` stratum, and the `max(1, …)` floor means the stratum is never sampled down to zero, however small the lane is in a month. Pinned by `tests/Feature/Correction13c01CalidadSamplesLaneAnswersTest.php`. No pre-pilot ticket is needed for sampling; the reviewer's *wrong* verdict opens the usual fix card. (The fix surface for a wrong lane answer is the glossary once 13f exists; until then the card is the record.)
- **NEG-11 — audit-only bypass, recorded.** On the forced-lane negatives for the expired-convenio profile, NEG-11 ("¿Me dejan irme antes si tengo médico?") produced a draft that passed both live locks (post-check, shape check). The independent audit flagged `spelled_number` on "una reducción de jornada". There was no digit, no figure and no entitlement wording, and in production pre-screen v2 refuses that question before the lane opens (`prescreen_v2_blocked`), so the draft would never be produced. It counts as one bypass under the gate's "0 bypasses" wording and is recorded here as audit-only, the same class as the earlier benign `una jornada` hit. The miss profile ran clean (43 turns, 0 bypasses, 0 figures).
