# ADR-0029 — Escalation explanations: deterministic facts, AI prose over them, employees never see the reason

**Status:** accepted (Sprint 7g Item 1)

## Context

Sprint 7f closed the last known confident-wrong-answer hole in the group-scope ladder by teaching the answer path to **escalate** in strictly more cases — an ambiguous sub-area, an unapproved group tree, a fact whose scope split after it was bound. That is the correct behaviour (§8.5, ADR-0028), but it has a cost 7f did not pay down: every one of those escalations, and every escalation from every earlier sprint (guardrail baseline, low-confidence floor, salary/reference-fact coverage gaps, the publish conflict fence), produced the **same generic per-reason copy** — one string per `reason` enum value, shown to both HR and, in spirit, implied to the employee. Two different problems hid behind that one flat text:

- **HR could not act on a card without re-deriving the trace by hand.** `low_confidence` covers at least nine structurally distinct failure modes (no retrieval, weak retrieval, a failed citation check, an ungrounded figure, a failed entailment check on a *named* claim, a truncated grounding retry, the aggregation guard, the salary/prose cross-path, a missing/failing provider) — each with a different fix, and none of it was surfaced. An agent working the board had to open the trace panel and reconstruct which of those nine actually fired, every time.
- **The employee-facing text varied by reason, which is itself a faint signal.** Before 7g, the copy shown in chat differed slightly per escalation reason. None of it leaked anything of real consequence, but the variance meant an attentive employee could in principle infer *something* about why (a low-confidence phrasing vs. an off-domain phrasing), and there was no test asserting the text was reason-independent — a future addition to any reason's copy could silently introduce a real leak (a document id, a convenio name, a hint that "this touches another employee's record").

The obvious fix for the first problem — let an LLM read the trace and write HR a summary — reintroduces exactly the failure mode this whole platform is built to avoid: a model **inventing** a plausible-sounding reason not actually supported by the trace, on a legal-weight review surface. The obvious fix for the second — leave employee copy untouched — misses a real opportunity to close a variance nobody had asserted shut.

## Decision

Two independent moves, one per side of the escalation, and a hard rule connecting them: **nothing generated by AI is ever the thing a fix acts on.**

### 1. Employee side — one fixed neutral message, asserted by a full-enum scan

Every escalation, regardless of `reason` or the sub-outcome inside it, shows the employee exactly one string:

> *"Un/a compañero/a de Recursos Humanos revisará tu consulta y te responderá."*

`ChatService::EMPLOYEE_ESCALATION_MESSAGE` replaces every reason-specific copy at the single point `persistTurn()` writes the employee-visible answer; every call site still constructs its own internal copy for the trace/HR side (kept as call-site documentation of *why* that path escalated), but only the fixed constant ever reaches `chat_messages.content` for an escalated turn. `EmployeeEscalationMessageScanTest` drives every reason the guardrail baseline, router, salary path, reference-fact path, and composition path can produce and asserts the employee text is **identical** across all of them and contains **no** reason token, document id, convenio name, or another person's name. This is a **narrowing** relative to the pre-7g behaviour, not a new capability — and narrowing the employee side while *widening* the HR side is the point: the axis that must carry more information is HR's, never the employee's.

### 2. HR side — two layers, and the AI layer is structurally incapable of deciding anything

**Layer 1 — deterministic facts (`EscalationExplainer::explain(reason, trace)`), no provider call.** A pure function: reason + the trace already recorded on the turn → structured facts (`asked`, `found`, `stopped_reason`, `fix_action`, `fix_surface`, `fix_link`, `employee_told`). It reads *only* fields already written to the trace by the deterministic pipeline (`architecture.md §5`/§7) — it introduces no new signal, it just **names** signal that already existed but was never surfaced. `EscalationExplainer::MATRIX` enumerates every `reason.sub_outcome` pair the live pipeline can produce (39 entries, spanning the pre-retrieval guardrail baseline and off-domain, the answer-or-escalate floor's eleven sub-gates, the fact-vs-convenio conflict, both coverage-gap families split by their real sub-causes, and the publish-time 409 reasons) and `EscalationExplainerGuardTest` walks the matrix, **failing the build** if `registry()` lacks an entry for any of them. This is the same discipline as the Sprint-6 `stricter_of` combination and the Sprint-7a tag-proposal invariants: a single point that cannot silently regress as the enum grows, enforced by a test rather than a review habit.

**Layer 2 — an AI paragraph over Layer 1's output only, verified against it, and demoted on any doubt.** `EscalationExplanationService` calls hr-ai's **existing** `/synthesise` endpoint with `ROUTER_MODEL` (Haiku) — the same cheap-model, same-key-path convention `RouterService` already uses for `/route` (`architecture.md §5`/§7; hr-ai gains no new endpoint and no new code at all). The "source" it synthesises from is not a document chunk; it is Layer 1's facts, rendered one-per-line, with a fixed instruction: restate these in plain HR Spanish, in one short paragraph, and add nothing. This is deliberately the narrowest possible AI task — paraphrase, not judgement — and it is checked as such: `EscalationExplanationGuard` is a deterministic post-hoc verifier (no second LLM call) that confirms every fact value (names, figures, the fix text) appears in the returned paragraph, and that the paragraph does not introduce a number or a name that isn't in the facts. **On any failure** — no answer-model key configured, a provider/transport error, or a failed no-new-claims check — `explanation_text` stays `null` and the card falls back to `EscalationExplainer::factsToSentences()`, the same facts rendered as plain deterministic sentences. A card is never left showing an unverified AI claim, and it is never held open waiting on the provider (the call runs **asynchronously**, queued after the card-creation transaction commits — the same posture `ProposeDocumentTags`/`SegmentReferenceSource` already take). Cost is logged per card via the existing `trace_fragment.cost_usd` convention.

**The rule that makes Layer 2 safe regardless of the guard's coverage: `fix_action`/`fix_surface`/`fix_link` are always Layer 1's values.** The paragraph is labelled "Resumen IA" and is *only* prose — the button HR clicks ("Corregir") and the surface/link it opens are read from the deterministic columns every time, never from anything the model wrote. Even a no-new-claims check that let something slip through could not misroute a fix, because the fix was never the model's to write.

### 3. The fix needs somewhere to point — the deep-link scheme (Item 2, folded in because Item 1 depends on it)

A fix that only names a surface ("go to the Directory") without a link back to the exact row is a fix HR has to re-search for. The existing `#doc=<uuid>` compat form (Sprint 7e) is generalised, additively, to:

```
#view=<view>&tab=<tab>&fact=<uuid>&emp=<uuid>&convenio=<id>
```

`AdminShell` reads this on mount and on `hashchange`, so a "Corregir" click from an already-open card (no page reload) still navigates. `fix_link` values use it directly: `#view=directory&emp=<uuid>` for a missing/wrong employee scope field, `#view=review&tab=reference-facts&fact=<uuid>` for a fact that needs re-binding or verification, `#view=review&tab=groups&convenio=<id>` for an unapproved group tree, and so on. A reason with no system-side fix (sensitive topic, legal/medical, other-employee-data, the aggregation guard, the salary/prose cross-path) carries `fix_link = null` and names the human action instead ("respond directly by another channel") — the schema does not force a link where none is meaningful.

### 4. Storage — five additive, nullable columns; no reshape

`escalation_cards` gains `explanation_facts` (jsonb), `explanation_text` (text), `fix_action`, `fix_surface`, `fix_link` (varchar) — all nullable, computed once at (or shortly after) card creation, never recomputed on read. Every pre-7g card reads all five `null` until `escalations:backfill-explanations` runs (a command, deliberately not a migration-time backfill — a migration is schema, not a data-population job with retry/report semantics). The sprint spec described this as "the four card columns"; the shipped shape is five (`explanation_facts` and `explanation_text` are two separate columns, not one, so the deterministic layer and the AI layer can independently be null) — noted here rather than silently reconciled, because the extra column is exactly the boundary this ADR is about: the deterministic layer must be able to exist, complete, with the AI layer still null.

## Consequences

- **One additive migration** (`escalation_cards` gains the five columns above), no CHECK rewrite, no enum change. hr-ai gains **no new endpoint** — the Haiku call reuses `/synthesise` exactly as `/route` already reuses the same provider path, which is the sprint constraint stated and honoured, not merely satisfied by omission. hr-ai does gain one small, disclosed exception: `ClaudeProvider.synthesise()`'s `trace_fragment` now includes `cost_usd` (mirroring the cost computation `/propose-groups`/`/ocr-page` already do, plus a `claude-haiku-4-5` pricing entry checked against Anthropic's published rate card) — needed because the spec requires cost logged per card, and pricing has never lived in hr-backend. A computed field on an existing response, not a new endpoint or a new decision; every existing `/synthesise` caller (the employee prose-answer path) already ignores unknown `trace_fragment` keys.
- **No change to any escalation decision, gate, or answer.** `Sprint7cAdditivityRegressionTest` and the 7c/7f reference-fact suites are byte-for-byte unchanged; this ADR governs only what is *displayed* after a decision already made elsewhere, never the decision itself.
- **The guard test is the durable guarantee, not a one-time audit.** A future sprint that adds a tenth `low_confidence` sub-gate, or a new publish-time 409 reason, will fail `EscalationExplainerGuardTest` the moment it lands unexplained — the same shape as the Sprint-6 `GuardrailPolicy` and the Sprint-7a tag-proposal invariant tests: safety properties enforced by a test that runs on every build, not by a habit of remembering to update a table.
- **The AI paragraph can regress to the deterministic sentences at any time, silently, and that is intended.** A provider outage, a rotated/missing key, or a paraphrase that slips a number does not degrade the card to "nothing" — it degrades to the same facts, written plainly. The worst-case HR experience is "less pleasant prose," never "wrong or missing information."
- **The employee narrowing is the more important of the two changes, and it is the one with no user-visible upside.** It closes a variance nobody had noticed was a variance; the value is a closed door, not a new feature, and the full-enum scan test is what keeps a future reason addition from silently reopening it.
- **The deep-link scheme is now load-bearing for more than Item 1.** `fix_link` is only its first consumer; any future admin surface that wants to point somewhere specific reuses the same hash, rather than inventing another per-surface scheme.
- Cites **ADR-0007** (hr-backend owns writes; the provider-call convention `RouterService` established for `/route`, reused verbatim here), **ADR-0011/0020** (AI proposes, human verifies — the same spine, applied here to *prose* rather than to a *fact*: the AI's paragraph is verified against the facts before it is shown, the same way an AI-proposed tag is inert until a human confirms it), **ADR-0015/0016** (the provider key path; fail-safe on provider error), **ADR-0023/0024/0028** (the escalation reasons this explainer covers, and the trace fields it reads).
